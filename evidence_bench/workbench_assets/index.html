Evidence and recorded review state

    Sources are shown as text; this workbench does not fetch them.

    Changing the response, prompt, or outcome resets judgments for review. Refusal and clarification content axes are not applicable. A missing outcome requires empty response text.

    Judgment guide

    Correct: reviewed claims and required citations are supported, or the answer/refusal/clarification decision is appropriate to the case.

    Incorrect: a material contradiction, unsupported assertion, fabricated or required-but-missing citation, or inappropriate response decision.

    Unscored: unknown or not assessed. Not applicable: the axis is outside the response scope; it is not a passing score.

    These labels are entered by the reviewer. The engine checks schema consistency and calculates metrics; it does not decide truth.
